Why this platform exists

One coordinated home for supporters, coordinators and party administrators.

Every registered supporter is mapped to their exact state, LGA and ward — so organizing stays local, verifiable and accountable.

01

Verified membership

Every account is tied to a validated voter's card, so the network reflects real, eligible supporters rather than anonymous noise.

02

Ward-level structure

Supporters are organized by state → LGA → ward, mirroring the actual structure coordinators use on the ground.

03

Direct communication

Announcements, updates and direct messages flow between administrators and supporters — no middlemen, no rumours.

Getting started

Registration takes four steps

  • 1
    Basic details Name, phone number, position, and your state / LGA / ward.
  • 2
    Voter's card verification Enter your 19-character VIN and upload a photo of your card.
  • 3
    Automatic matching The VIN you typed is checked against the VIN read from your card image.
  • 4
    Set a password & complete profile Secure your account and add a profile picture to unlock the home feed.
